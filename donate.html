About me GitHub

Hi, I'm Prem Kumar!

I'm a Salesforce developer and the maintainer of MYNT (Material You New Tab), a new tab extension for Chrome and Firefox, and SIR Plus, a fork of Salesforce Inspector Reloaded with numerous bug fixes, enhancements, and improvements. I also build browser extensions and open-source tools that make everyday work a little smoother.

I care about the details: the edge cases, the small imperfections, and getting things right instead of just getting them to work. Your support helps me:

  • Maintain and improve MYNT, SIR Plus and my other extensions.
  • Build new features based on community feedback.
  • Keep sharing my work through open-source projects.

Every contribution, big or small, helps keep these tools free and open to everyone. Thank you for your support!
